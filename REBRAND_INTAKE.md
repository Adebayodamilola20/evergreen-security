# Rebrand Intake — What we need from you (the client)

We're rebuilding this website under your **new company name**. Right now the site is built for another brand, so all the names, photos, text, and contact details need to be replaced with yours. Please send the items below. Anything you don't have yet, just say so and we'll note it and move on.

**Easiest way to send:** one shared Google Drive / Dropbox folder for the photos, plus one document (Google Doc or Word) with the written content using the headings below.

---

## 1. Brand basics

- [ ] **Company name** — exact spelling and capitalization, as it should appear everywhere.
- [ ] **Legal/registered business name** (if different).
- [ ] **Tagline / slogan** — one short line.
- [ ] **A one-sentence description** of the company (used in the browser tab and Google search result).
- [ ] **Brand colors** — if you have them.
- [ ] **Domain name** you'll use (for email addresses and launch).

## 2. Logo

- [ ] **Main logo** — highest quality you have, ideally on a transparent background.
- [ ] **A light/white version** of the logo — the top menu bar is dark, so we need one that shows on dark.
- [ ] **Favicon** — the small icon in the browser tab (we can make this from your logo if needed).

## 3. Photos

Send real photos where you can — guards on duty, patrols, control room, sites, team, events. Wide/landscape shots for banners; clean head-and-shoulders portraits for the leadership team. Highest quality you have.

- [ ] A strong **homepage banner** — a short company video, or one large hero photo.
- [ ] A handful of **operational photos** for the homepage (guards working, patrol, coverage).
- [ ] **Portrait photos of each leader** for the team page.
- [ ] A set of your **best photos for the gallery** page.
- [ ] A **banner image for each blog article**.

> Please only send photos you own or have permission to use, and confirm anyone shown agreed to appear on the site.

## 4. Leadership team

For each leader you want featured:

- [ ] Full name
- [ ] Job title / role
- [ ] Short bio
- [ ] Longer bio
- [ ] Key credentials / achievements
- [ ] Certifications & professional affiliations (if any)
- [ ] Social media links (optional)
- [ ] Photo

## 5. About the company

- [ ] **Your story** — how the company came about / how it started.
- [ ] **Mission, vision, and core values.**
- [ ] **Where you're based / where you operate** — your locations and the areas or regions you serve.

## 6. Services & who you serve

- [ ] **Your services** — the list you offer, with a short description of each.
- [ ] **Industries you serve** — the types of clients/sites you protect.
- [ ] **Training** — describe your training program (what guards are trained on).
- [ ] **Quality assurance** — how you keep service standards high (supervision, checks, standards).
- [ ] **Overseas / international services** — if you operate or serve clients abroad, describe it. If not, tell us and we'll remove that page.
- [ ] **Company numbers** — real figures for the counters on the homepage (e.g. years in operation, guards deployed, clients served, sites protected).

## 7. Testimonials (client reviews)

- [ ] A few short client quotes, each with the person's name and their role & company. If you can't share real names, tell us and we'll anonymize them.

## 8. Blog articles

- [ ] Either send us your articles (title + a few paragraphs each), or tell us the topics and we'll draft them for your approval.

## 9. Careers / job applications

- [ ] Are you currently hiring? If so, the roles and any requirements.
- [ ] Where should job applications go (email), or should the on-site form email you directly?

## 10. Contact details

- [ ] **Phone number(s).**
- [ ] **Email address(es).**
- [ ] **Physical address(es)** — full address for each office/location.
- [ ] **Business hours.**
- [ ] **Social media links** — whichever you have.
- [ ] **Map location** — a Google Maps link or exact address for the map pin.

---

### Where to start
Send the **company name, logo, and brand colors** first — that lets us rebrand the whole site right away. Then the **about, leadership, and contact details**, then **photos**, then the rest. You don't have to send everything at once; give us what you have and we'll build as it comes in.
